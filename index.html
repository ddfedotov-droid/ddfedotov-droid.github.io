DF agents

A private, self-hosted personal finance tool. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads transaction-alert emails from the owner's own bank, in the owner's own Gmail account, with read-only access.
  • Turns those alerts into a spending ledger that is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Shows the ledger in a password-protected dashboard that runs on that computer.

It never sends, deletes, or changes email, and it cannot move money.
